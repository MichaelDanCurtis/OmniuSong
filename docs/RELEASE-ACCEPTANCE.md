# Native Rust release acceptance

October 20, 2026. The complete objective in [GOAL.md](GOAL.md) is the release contract. This matrix records evidence; a prototype result does not qualify the Rust replacement. Every row must pass its applicable Mac and Windows checks before the goal is complete.

States: **pending** means no native proof; **in progress** means implementation or qualification is underway; **passed** requires a linked artifact covering the whole row. Device, listening, installer and platform checks are independent gates. A documented limitation does not turn a required feature into a pass.

Full milestone 2 and every release row remain incomplete. The [current audio source gate](verification/rust-native-capture-recovery-tests.json) has 278 passing entries, zero failures and one intentionally ignored helper, plus five focused vendor tests; it does not close the integrated recording, hardware, listening, installer or Windows requirements. The [earlier 191-entry Mac walkthrough](verification/rust-native-disconnect-ui.json) covers backup/copy, paired edit/history, malformed-backup rejection and confirmed process exit/relaunch/reopen with recording details and recovery disclosure retained.

## Baseline evidence

[Read-only baseline inventory](verification/rust-migration-baseline.json) records 19 schema-1 projects and 11 audio assets before migration. Existing preview qualification remains in STATUS and its linked receipts. Original project/audio hashes must still match after native migration qualification. The unrelated `.serena/` working-tree entry is preserved.

[Initial native tests](verification/rust-native-tests.json) fingerprint that earlier source and its logs; [native UI evidence](verification/rust-native-ui.json) identifies the actual development binary observed. These are separate scopes: headless tests do not prove installed UI behavior, and a responsive loading window does not prove studio storage, devices, inference or release readiness.

The [earlier disconnect follow-up source gate](verification/rust-native-disconnect-tests.json) records 191 passing entries, clean formatting/Clippy, a rebuilt Mac bundle and verified development signature. The [176-entry session/copy gate](verification/rust-native-session-copy.json), [152-entry take/archive gate](verification/rust-native-takes.json), [schema-2 inspection UI](verification/rust-native-recording-ui.json) and [pre-disconnect UI receipt](verification/rust-native-session-copy-ui.json) remain historical evidence for their named sources/binaries. The pre-disconnect UI completed its first export/inspection/copy, paired Save/Undo/Redo and dirty-navigation guards; drive loss interrupted the second export, and restart was not qualified on that binary. The fresh schema-4 import covers all 19 originals, and all 30 initial source hashes remain unchanged. The earlier synthetic archive fixture retained 33 MiB PCM and 5,120 events at 21,839,872 bytes maximum process RSS; filesystem cache, actual capture, physical timing/listening and 30-minute stability remain outside that measurement.

[Recovery qualification](verification/rust-native-disconnect-recovery.json) and [promotion evidence](verification/rust-native-disconnect-promotion.json) retain all four current documents after the actual drive loss. Three complete projects are exact; the fourth is a fresh revision-0 fork of surviving revision-8 music with source/evidence embedded. One history row and three old operation receipts were lost, no old admissions were fabricated, and the raw damaged store remains retained. This manual incident recovery and typed `IOERR`/`CORRUPT`/`NOTADB` quarantine do not qualify wrapped/per-project errors, stalled-I/O deadlines, persistent volume enrollment, general recovery UI or physical power-loss survival.

The [rebuilt Mac walkthrough](verification/rust-native-disconnect-ui.json) exported/inspected/restored two copy generations, saved paired name/tempo edits with one Undo/Redo, rejected a 36-byte malformed backup without changing the selected session, and reopened the second-generation copy at revision 4 / 122.5 BPM after confirmed process exit/relaunch. Recording details and the recovery notice persisted. Independent backend comparisons retain the original four projects/rows, exact copied music/notices/source JSON/embedded archive and the immutable take; all 30 prototype hashes and the source/binary/candidate/raw evidence match. This is actual development UI/storage evidence; audio, MIDI, inference, plugins, installers and Windows remain unqualified. Low light-theme warning contrast and stale background-work status after failed inspection remain observed H07/H08 issues.
