# Native performance tap

Status: first library packet for milestone 2 saved keyboard/pad performance capture. This contract does not implement a recording command, retained journal writer, project attachment or editable performance projection. The separate [capture architecture](../reviews/RUST-PERFORMANCE-CAPTURE-ARCHITECTURE.md) governs those remaining stages.

`recording_live_pair` constructs the existing procedural live renderer with one unique off-callback tap owner. `live_pair` remains ordinary unsaved audition. Neither constructor opens a device. The current native worker and UI still use ordinary audition; no new record control or IPC profile is exposed by this packet.

Each callback reserves its entire worst-case transfer before applying events. Fixed Copy packets carry Begin, at most four complete batch originals with their actual disposition, contiguous logical F32 PCM, and an End fence. The ring holds 256 packets; each packet is at most 16 KiB and the ring payload at most 4 MiB. One invocation has at most 16,384 frames, eight channels, 64 voices, 128 automatic transitions and 70 transferred packets. PCM is split into 256-frame packets. There is no heap owner, filesystem access or consumer mutex operation in the renderer transfer path.

Source observations, worker acceptance and callback application remain separate. A full rejected or Panic-discarded batch is preserved without interpreting an invalid length. Applied sequence evidence follows whole-batch application. Native integer output conversion occurs after the tapped F32 mix. These PCM values describe the logical renderer bus; they establish neither physical delivery nor what a person heard.

Local event origins, connection generations, complete 16-bit velocities, source ticks, patches, repeated voice identities, sustain and Pulse durations remain exact. Local GUI events do not become MIDI packets. The tap does not infer a source-clock denominator, host/audio alignment, MIDI channel or project tick. Automatic Pulse releases and voice ends have their own frame receipts; Panic and fault clears are aggregate engine transitions rather than invented human NoteOffs. A fault cause still being published is retained as FaultPending at the actual clear frame and is not re-timed when its cause becomes visible.

Overflow, cancellation, receiver loss or invalid transfer faults permanently close the tap. No subsequent Begin resumes it. Previously queued originals can still drain. A transfer interruption can leave a Begin/PCM prefix without End; completion counters do not cover that partial group. Cancellation sampled off the callback retains a coherent last-completed prefix when available; a busy publication omits the callback index rather than correlating mixed observations. End means one complete callback transfer, never durable storage, a sealed take or complete capture of everything accepted by the parent.

The off-callback admission owner must outlive the complete render and native error closures. The renderer explicitly destroys its entire recording producer wrapper before acknowledging render destruction. Producer-destroyed alone is not native quiescence. Broken premature retention ownership deliberately keeps one bounded backing allocation until process exit rather than allowing the realtime callback to free the queue. The future worker must apply the existing two-closure retirement rule before releasing the admission owner.

The [source review](../reviews/RUST-PERFORMANCE-TAP-SOURCE-REVIEW.md) and [software qualification](../verification/rust-native-performance-tap.json) retain the bounded tests and actual fixture. Native devices, sustained load, listening, timing calibration, journal durability, Start/Finish/Stop, recovery, attachment/history/archive and editable projection need their own acceptance. Preserve the complete performed PCM and event originals before deriving editable 960 PPQ notes, since the current live and saved audition renderers differ.
