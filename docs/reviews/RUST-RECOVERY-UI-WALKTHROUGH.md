## Actionable UI concerns and limits

- **Pre-/post-recovery wording is mixed.** `recovered()` retains the reviewed inspection unchanged. The panel consequently displays its old Recording/Interrupted and **No sealed manifest** text above a new sealed receipt. Capture both during acceptance; the receipt is the acknowledged result. A follow-up should label the old block “Before recovery / reviewed evidence” or otherwise separate it from current sealed state.
- **Retained does not mean unattached.** Stage discovery does not resolve project attachment membership, and publication does not remove a stage. A sealed stage can remain listed after successful attachment/restart. A follow-up attachment-status cue would reduce accidental duplicate choices; this walkthrough must inspect the saved track before considering another attachment.
- **Scroll and focus need actual checks.** The right panel is narrow and contains an outer review scroll area plus a virtualized stage list. Long paths/digests and retained cards can push Recover/Attach/Retry below the visible area. Scroll the correct pane before activation; an AX node's presence alone is not proof that a visible click or keyboard focus works. Verify tab/focus order, selector access and light/dark readability separately.
- **Only the first 16 diagnostics are rendered.** Additional scan/source problems have a count but no expansion control. A larger diagnostic set cannot be fully reviewed through this UI; retain its backend report and leave full diagnostic accessibility open.
- **Retry/rejected-choice cards are window state.** They retain full exact intents and evidence in memory, with up to eight released rejected choices; they are not a durable user retry inbox. At that bound, keep-local still releases the session guard, but another rejected choice cannot be released in that window. Closing does not preserve the UI card itself or prove cancellation. Journal manifests/receipts and project ledgers are separate durable evidence; after restart inspect before making another attachment choice.
- **Do not close during the main happy-path operations.** Close guards offer an explicit close-without-waiting choice and do not promise rollback or completion. Testing that interruption belongs in isolated software tests, not this live-catalog walkthrough. Physical capture, audible quality, latency, long-duration continuity, drive/power-loss, MIDI, plugins and inference remain outside this review.

No new implementation is proposed or applied by this review. The coordinator owns actual actions, acceptance disposition and the final evidence receipt.
